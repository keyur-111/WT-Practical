About Greenfield College

Greenfield College is a friendly institution focused on practical learning, student support, and career readiness. Our programs are designed to help students build skills for the workplace.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a college where every student gains confidence, useful skills, and a strong start to their career.

Mission

To offer quality courses, supportive teaching, and industry links that help students succeed.

Program Specific Outcomes

PSO 1

Apply subject knowledge to practical and real-world problems.

PSO 2

Work well in teams and communicate ideas clearly.

PSO 3

Use tools and methods appropriate for modern workplaces.
